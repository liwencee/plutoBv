Companionship Care

Because no one should have no one

Regular company and conversation for people who would otherwise spend long stretches of time alone.

What's included

  • Regular social visits
  • Accompanying clients to appointments or on errands
  • Shared hobbies and activities
  • Help staying connected with family and friends
  • A consistent, familiar face, not a rotating roster

Who it's for

People living alone, recently bereaved, or at risk of isolation, where company matters as much as physical care.
