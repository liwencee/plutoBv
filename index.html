Our Commitments

What you can hold us to

Plutobv is a young agency, so rather than borrow someone else's words, here is what we guarantee on every placement. Ask us to evidence any of it.

Vetted before we ever send them

Enhanced DBS check and UK right-to-work verification on every member of staff, with references followed up rather than filed. We will show you the paperwork for anyone we place with you.

Trained before the first shift

Structured training completed up front, not an induction chat on the way out of the door. Where a placement needs something specific, such as autism-informed support or medication handling, we match on that, not just availability.

The same faces, and one number to call

We keep the same worker with the same client wherever we can, because continuity is most of what good care feels like. You get one named account manager, reachable out of hours, not a rota of strangers.

Work Process

How it works

  • Stringent vetting and screening, including DBS checks and UK right-to-work verification.
  • One point of contact covering a full range of patient conditions and care needs.
  • Coverage across social care disciplines, including NHS, care homes, and supported living.
  • A care-standards-compliant service from first enquiry to placement.
  • Experience managing TUPE transfers from an outgoing supplier's staff.
  • A dedicated account manager for fast responses when plans change.
  • Straightforward invoicing and review documentation, on your schedule.
