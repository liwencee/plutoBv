Health and social care staffing you can rely on

Plutobv recruits, vets, and places health care assistants and support workers across the public and private sector — supplying local authorities, the NHS, and private residential homes with staff who show up ready to care.

How We Do It

Supercharging your care workforce

Client-first matching

We start with your needs and requirements, then match staff to the specific people and settings they'll be working with.

Properly trained staff

Every care and support worker we place completes structured training before their first shift, not just an induction chat.

Flexible, reliable cover

Skilled, friendly staff willing to work flexible hours, so you're covered when you need it — not just during office hours.

What We're Offering

Care staffing across every setting

Live-in Care

Round-the-clock support in the comfort of your own home, so you can keep living life on your terms.

Domiciliary Care

Visiting support with daily tasks and personal care, helping people stay independent in their own homes.

Companionship Care

Regular company and conversation for people who'd otherwise spend long stretches of time alone.

Autism Support

Work Process

How it works

  • Stringent vetting and screening, including DBS checks and UK right-to-work verification.
  • One point of contact covering a full range of patient conditions and care needs.
  • Coverage across social care disciplines, including NHS, care homes, and supported living.
  • A care-standards-compliant service from first enquiry to placement.
  • Experience managing TUPE transfers from an outgoing supplier's staff.
  • A dedicated account manager for fast responses when plans change.
  • Straightforward invoicing and review documentation, on your schedule.
