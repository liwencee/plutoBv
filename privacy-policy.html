Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Who we are

This policy covers personal information submitted through plutobv.co.uk. For any question about it, contact us at info@plutobv.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect what you submit through our forms:

  • Contact form: name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Apply Now form: name, email address, phone number, role applied for, your message, and your CV file.
  • Timesheet form: name, staff reference, week ending date, your uploaded timesheet file, and any notes.

Why we collect it

To respond to enquiries, to process job applications, and to process timesheet submissions from our staff. We do not use this information for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

How it's stored

Form submissions, including uploaded CV and timesheet files, are sent directly by email to our team and are not stored in a database or kept in permanent file storage on our website. Uploaded files exist only as email attachments.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or tracking scripts.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact info@plutobv.co.uk to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change too.
